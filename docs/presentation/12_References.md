## 1. Official NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Primary Sources

1. **NASA Human Research Program: Hazards of Human Spaceflight**
   - *URL:* https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/
   - *Description:* Foundational NASA framework categorizing the five major spaceflight hazards: Space Radiation, Isolation and Confinement, Distance from Earth, Gravity Fields, and Hostile/Closed Environments.
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* Direct architectural mapping of all 15 modules, risk categorization, and countermeasure matrix.

2. **NASA Human Research Program: Risks of Human Spaceflight**
   - *URL:* https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/
   - *Description:* Comprehensive registry of the 30+ official human spaceflight risks, detailing physiological mechanisms, operational impacts, and mitigation roadmaps across bone loss, cardiovascular deconditioning, SANS, and behavioral health.
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* SANS optical congestion modeling, cardiovascular fluid shift deconditioning, and bone mineral loss algorithms.

3. **NASA Human Research Program: Ongoing HRP Research & Studies**
   - *URL:* https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/
   - *Description:* Overview of active flagship investigations on the International Space Station, including CIPHER, exploration medical capabilities, and behavioral monitoring.
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* Research Hub integration, ongoing clinical research frontiers documentation, and countermeasure protocol alignment.

4. **NASA Technical Standard: Space Flight Human-System Standard (NASA-STD-3001)**
   - *Volume 1:* Crew Health (Rev B)
   - *Volume 2:* Human Factors, Habitability, and Environmental Health
   - *Description:* Mandated engineering and biomedical standards establishing permissible radiation exposure limits, cabin atmospheric compositions, exercise countermeasure minimums, and medical care requirements for exploration missions.
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* Environmental threshold limits (PCO2 < 2.0 mmHg, O2 20.5–21.5%, cabin pressure 101.3 kPa) and career radiation dose limits.

## 2. Canadian Space Agency (CSA) Primary Sources

5. **Canadian Space Agency: Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health**
   - *URL:* https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp
   - *Description:* Official CSA report on space radiation dynamics, biological dosimeters, personal bubble detectors, and chromosomal damage monitoring in microgravity.
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* Radiation dosimetry monitoring module, cumulative career mSv tracking, and radiation storm shelter protocols.

6. **Canadian Space Agency: Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?**
   - *URL:* https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/iss/science/why-do-we-conduct-science-experiments-in-space.asp
   - *Description:* Comprehensive review of ISS science experiments highlighting microgravity cardiovascular aging, bone density reduction, and Earth-health applications (osteoporosis, remote medicine).
   - *AstroCare Implementation:* Research Hub scientific showcase and terrestrial spin-off documentation.

## 3. Peer-Reviewed Aerospace Medicine & Machine Learning Literature

7. **Liu, F. T., Ting, K. M., & Zhou, Z. H. (2008).**
   - *Paper:* "Isolation Forest." *IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM)*, pp. 413-422.
   - *Relevance:* Theoretical and algorithmic foundation for AstroCare's unsupervised anomaly detection engine.

8. **Clement, G., & Sleney, J. (2019).**
   - *Book:* *Fundamentals of Space Medicine*. Springer Aerospace Technology.
   - *Relevance:* Physiological baseline norms and cardiovascular cephalad fluid redistribution kinetics.

9. **Patel, Z. S., et al. (2020).**
   - *Paper:* "Red risks for a journey to the red planet: The highest priority human health risks for a mission to Mars." *npj Microgravity*, 6(1), 1-13.
   - *Relevance:* Multidimensional risk evaluation parameters and autonomous medical co-pilot requirements for Mars exploration.

10. **Lawley, J. S., et al. (2017).**
    - *Paper:* "Lack of tissue buoyancy causes intracranial hypertension in spaceflight: Implications for Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome." *Journal of Physiology*, 595(6), 2115-2127.
    - *Relevance:* Mechanism linking zero-gravity hydrostatic gradients, elevated ICP, and SANS optic disc edema.
