# 09 — Countermeasure Protocols & Clinical Action Engine

---

## 1. The Three-Tier Countermeasure Structure

AstroCare does not output abstract medical probabilities. Every identified risk is paired with an operational, three-tier countermeasure protocol designed for both on-board crew and ground mission controllers.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│            THREE-TIER COUNTERMEASURE STRUCTURE         │
├────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 1: Immediate  │ Point-of-care actions executed by │
│ Response Protocol  │ the astronaut or on-board CMO     │
├────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 2: Preventive │ Scheduled regimen adjustments for │
│ Mission Protocol   │ long-term flight resilience       │
├────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 3: Medical    │ Ground Flight Surgeon clinical    │
│ Escalation Steps   │ diagnostic commands & telemedicine│
└────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Detailed Countermeasure Dictionaries by Hazard

### Hazard A: Space Radiation Countermeasures
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):**
  - Sound internal solar particle alert.
  - Direct all crew members to evacuate to designated storm-shelter module behind water-wall tanks and polyethylene radiation barrier.
  - Abort and stow all extravehicular activity (EVA) equipment.
- **Tier 2 (Preventive):**
  - Orient spacecraft service module or fuel tanks between crew quarters and incoming solar proton flux direction.
  - Maintain continuous bio-dosimetry logging (CSA bubble detectors and lymphocyte sampling).
- **Tier 3 (Flight Surgeon Protocol):**
  - Calculate cumulative Gy-equivalent tissue dose; cross-reference NASA-STD-3001 career exposure limits.
  - Prescribe antioxidant/radioprotective supportive regimens as indicated by flight clinical guidelines.

### Hazard B: Isolation & Confinement Countermeasures
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):**
  - Enforce mandatory 2-hour sleep buffer; deconflict high-workload robotic or piloting schedules.
  - Shift crew quarter lighting to 2700K blue-depleted spectrum.
- **Tier 2 (Preventive):**
  - Implement structured sleep hygiene: enforce 8.0-hour sleep window at 22:00 UTC daily.
  - Conduct weekly private psychological video conferences with family and flight psychologist.
- **Tier 3 (Flight Surgeon Protocol):**
  - Review 7-day Psychomotor Vigilance Task (PVT) cognitive reaction times.
  - Evaluate non-habit-forming sleep interventions (melatonin/Ramelteon protocol).

### Hazard C: Distance from Earth Countermeasures
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):**
  - Transition AstroCare to **Autonomous Medical Decision Support (AMDS)** mode.
  - Designate Crew Medical Officer (CMO) as primary on-board medical authority.
- **Tier 2 (Preventive):**
  - Conduct monthly autonomous emergency medical simulations without ground intervention.
  - Maintain verified inventory of autonomous point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) and emergency trauma kits.
- **Tier 3 (Flight Surgeon Protocol):**
  - Transmit asynchronous clinical guidance consultation packets with annotated ultrasound imagery.

### Hazard D: Gravity Fields (Microgravity) Countermeasures
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):**
  - Position astronaut in neutral body posture with feet anchored to relieve back pain from spinal elongation.
  - If acute orthostatic hypotension occurs, inflate lower-body compression garments (G-suit).
- **Tier 2 (Preventive):**
  - Daily 2.5-hour countermeasure protocol: 60 minutes Advanced Resistive Exercise Device (ARED) heavy squats/deadlifts (minimum 150 kg axial load) + 30 minutes cycle ergometer interval training.
  - Dietary supplementation of 800 IU Vitamin D3 and 1000 mg Calcium daily.
- **Tier 3 (Flight Surgeon Protocol):**
  - Conduct monthly in-flight calcaneal ultrasound bone scans; monitor 24-hour urine calcium-to-creatinine ratio to prevent nephrolithiasis.

### Hazard E: Hostile / Closed Environments Countermeasures
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):**
  - In the event of acute hypoxia or hypercapnia, don portable emergency breathing mask supplying 100% O2.
  - Turn all Inter-Module Ventilation (IMV) fans to maximum speed to eliminate stagnant CO2 pockets.
- **Tier 2 (Preventive):**
  - Regenerate Carbon Dioxide Removal Assembly (CDRA) desiccant beds on 12-hour alternating cycles.
  - Maintain cabin ambient PCO2 strictly $< 2.0$ mmHg and total pressure at $101.3 \pm 1.5$ kPa.
- **Tier 3 (Flight Surgeon Protocol):**
  - Perform near-point visual acuity sweep and Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) retinal nerve fiber layer sweep if headache or visual blurring persists.
