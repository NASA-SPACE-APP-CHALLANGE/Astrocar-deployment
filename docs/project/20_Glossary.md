# AstroCare – Exhaustive Aerospace Biomedical & Technical Glossary

### A
- **ARED (Advanced Resistive Exercise Device)**: The primary strength-training apparatus aboard the International Space Station, utilizing inertial flywheel vacuum cylinders to provide up to 2,600 N (600 lbf) of constant load to counteract muscle atrophy and bone mineral density loss.
- **Arrhythmia**: Any variation from the normal rhythm and sequence of electrical activation of the heart muscle, monitored in spaceflight due to electrolyte shifts and autonomic nervous system recalibration.
- **Artemis Program**: NASA-led international human spaceflight program aiming to land the first woman and next man on the Moon, establish sustainable lunar surface exploration, and prepare for human missions to Mars.

### B
- **Bio-Monitor**: A smart wearable garment developed by the Canadian Space Agency (CSA) equipped with sensors to continuously record vital signs (ECG, respiration, blood pressure, skin temp, activity) during astronaut daily missions without disrupting crew tasks.
- **Bradycardia**: A heart rate resting below 60 beats per minute.

### C
- **Cephalad Fluid Shift**: The upward redistribution of approximately 1.5 to 2 liters of intravascular and interstitial bodily fluids from the lower extremities toward the thorax and head caused by the absence of hydrostatic gravity vectors in spaceflight.
- **CHAPEA (Crew Health and Performance Exploration Analog)**: A NASA ground-based analog mission series involving four crew members residing in a 1,700-square-foot 3D-printed habitat at Johnson Space Center for 378 days to simulate Mars surface life.
- **Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)**: An active health information technology system designed to provide physicians, flight surgeons, or crew members with person-specific assessments and evidence-based recommendations to augment medical decisions.

### D
- **Decompression Sickness (DCS)**: A condition caused by inert gas bubbles (predominantly nitrogen) coming out of physical solution in blood and tissue when ambient cabin or suit pressure drops too rapidly during EVA preparations.
- **DSN (Deep Space Network)**: NASA's international array of giant radio antennas located in California (Goldstone), Spain (Madrid), and Australia (Canberra) that supports interplanetary spacecraft missions.

### E
- **ECLSS (Environmental Control and Life Support System)**: The integrated aerospace engineering hardware suite responsible for providing clean breathing air, maintaining cabin pressure, controlling temperature and humidity, removing carbon dioxide, and recycling wastewater.
- **EVA (Extravehicular Activity)**: Any work or spacewalk operation performed by an astronaut outside a protective spacecraft or lunar/planetary habitat in a pressurized space suit.

### G
- **Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR)**: High-energy protons and atomic nuclei (HZE ions) originating from outside the Solar System, possessing extreme penetrating power and creating significant secondary ionizing radiation cascades.
- **Gray (Gy)**: The SI unit of absorbed ionizing radiation dose, equivalent to one joule of radiation energy absorbed per kilogram of matter ($1\text{ Gy} = 1\text{ J/kg}$).

### H
- **HERA (Human Exploration Research Analog)**: A compact, three-story isolated habitat located at NASA's Johnson Space Center used to study psychological confinement, sleep deprivation, team dynamics, and operational autonomy during simulated 45-day spaceflights.
- **Hostile/Closed Environment**: One of NASA's five human spaceflight hazards, encompassing toxic chemical off-gassing, microbial mutation, elevated carbon dioxide, micro-particulate dust (lunar regolith), and life support mechanical breakdowns.
- **HRP (Human Research Program)**: NASA's dedicated biomedical research entity tasked with identifying, quantifying, mitigating, and monitoring the human risks of space exploration.
- **HRV (Heart Rate Variability)**: The physiological variation in the time interval between consecutive heartbeats (measured via RMSSD or SDNN), serving as a sensitive indicator of autonomic nervous system balance and physiological stress.
- **Hypercapnia**: Excess carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) in the bloodstream, commonly induced in spaceflight habitats when ambient cabin $CO_2$ exceeds 3.0–5.0 mmHg due to local stagnant air pockets in microgravity.
- **Hypoxia**: A state of oxygen deprivation at the tissue level, arising when ambient cabin oxygen falls below safe partial pressures or during suit pressure drops.

### I
- **Isolation and Confinement**: NASA hazard category encompassing psychological stress, circadian misalignment, sleep fragmentation, mood degradation, and neurocognitive exhaustion stemming from extended confinement in tight, sensory-deprived spacecraft.
- **Isolation Forest**: An unsupervised machine learning algorithm based on decision trees that isolates anomalies by recursively partitioning data points; anomalous instances require significantly fewer splits than normal clusters.

### L
- **LET (Linear Energy Transfer)**: A physical quantity measuring the amount of energy an ionizing particle transfers to the material traversed per unit distance ($dE/dx$, expressed in $\text{keV}/\mu\text{m}$). High-LET radiation (such as cosmic iron nuclei) causes dense, complex biological DNA damage.

### M
- **Microgravity ($\mu g$)**: The condition of apparent weightlessness experienced by persons and objects in continuous freefall orbit around a celestial body ($10^{-6}\text{ to }10^{-3} g$).

### N
- **NASA-STD-3001**: NASA Space Flight Human-System Standard, the official agency specification establishing baseline limits for human physiological health, environmental safety, biomedical monitoring, and clinical care capability.

### O
- **Orthostatic Intolerance**: The inability to maintain blood pressure and cerebral perfusion when returning to an upright posture in a gravity field (lunar, Martian, or terrestrial) after cardiovascular deconditioning in microgravity, frequently leading to presyncope or syncope.

### P
- **Photoplethysmography (PPG)**: An optical measurement technique used to illuminate skin tissue and measure volumetric variations in microvascular blood volume, commonly utilized in pulse oximeters to calculate pulse rate and oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$).

### R
- **RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences)**: A time-domain metric of heart rate variability reflecting high-frequency vagal (parasympathetic) modulation of heart rate.

### S
- **SANS (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome)**: A neuro-ophthalmic condition characterized by optic disc edema, globe flattening, choroidal folds, cotton wool spots, and hyperopic refractive shifts, driven by prolonged cephalad venous congestion and altered translaminar cribrosa pressure gradients.
- **Sievert (Sv)**: The SI unit of ionizing radiation equivalent dose and effective dose, measuring the stochastic health risk of ionizing radiation by weighting absorbed dose by radiation biological effectiveness ($1\text{ Sv} = 1\text{ J/kg} \times Q$).
- **Solar Particle Event (SPE)**: Intense fluxes of high-energy protons emitted by the Sun during coronal mass ejections (CMEs) and solar flares, posing acute radiation sickness risks to astronauts outside Earth's magnetosphere.
- **SpO2**: Peripheral capillary oxygen saturation, an estimate of the percentage of oxygenated hemoglobin molecules relative to total hemoglobin in arterial blood.

### T
- **Tachycardia**: An abnormally elevated resting heart rate exceeding 100 beats per minute in adult humans.
- **Telemetry**: The automated communication process by which biomedical and environmental measurements are collected at remote locations (e.g., inside a space suit or habitat) and transmitted to receiving equipment for monitoring.
